This Privacy Policy explains how Block Architect (package ID: com.blockarchitect.puzzle, referred to as the "Application", "App", "we", "us", or "our") handles, processes, stores, and protects information when you install and use our mobile puzzle game on Android devices.

By downloading, accessing, or playing Block Architect, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Application.

Summary at a Glance:
  • No User Accounts Required: We do not require you to register, log in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, or contacts to play Block Architect.
  • On-Device Game Progress: Your puzzle progress, stars, daily streaks, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob & UMP): The App displays banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob, which automatically collects standard device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), coarse IP-based location, ad interaction events, and crash/diagnostic data in accordance with Google's privacy policies and your consent choices.

1. Information We Collect and Store

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

Block Architect does not require user registration or account creation. We do not collect personal contact details such as your name, email address, physical address, or telephone number within the App. If you contact us voluntarily via email for customer support, we will only use your email address and message content to respond to your inquiry.

1.2 Local Game Progress & Preferences (On-Device Storage Only)

To allow you to resume your puzzles and keep your earned stars, the Application stores the following non-personal game data exclusively on your device using Android SharedPreferences and WebView localStorage:

  • Completed levels, star ratings (1★–3★), and best move counts.
  • Daily Building completion history and streak counters.
  • Active in-progress level board state (for automatic resume).
  • In-game preferences (selected language, sound effects toggle, background music toggle, speech bubbles toggle, and reduced motion setting).

This local gameplay data is never transmitted to our servers and can be erased at any time using the in-app Settings → Reset Progress button or by clearing the App's storage in your Android device settings.

1.3 Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob & UMP)

To keep Block Architect free to play, we integrate the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. These third-party SDKs provided by Google LLC may automatically collect and share certain device and usage data as disclosed below (matching our Google Play Data Safety declaration):

Data Category Specific Data Types Purpose of Collection & Sharing
Device or Other IDs Google Advertising ID (AAID), App Set ID, device make/model, operating system version, screen resolution, and device language. Advertising & marketing (serving personalized or non-personalized ads), frequency capping, analytics, and fraud prevention.
Location (Approximate) Coarse geographical location (country/region/city level) inferred automatically from your device's IP address. (The App never requests GPS or precise location permissions.) Selecting regionally relevant advertisements, regulatory compliance (GDPR/CCPA detection), and fraud prevention.
App Activity Ad views (impressions), ad clicks, rewarded video completions, and basic session interaction events with advertisements. Advertising measurement, attribution, rewarding in-game hints/bonus time, and fraud prevention.
App Info & Performance Crash logs, ANR (Application Not Responding) diagnostics, and SDK network performance metrics. App stability, SDK diagnostics, and reliability improvements.

3. Legal Basis, GDPR Consent (EEA / UK / Switzerland) & UMP

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom (UK), or Switzerland, we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the ePrivacy Directive by using Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK:

  • Upon your first launch in a regulated region, a consent form is presented allowing you to consent to or manage how Google and its ad technology partners process your data for personalized or non-personalized advertising.
  • Withdrawing or Changing Your Consent Anytime: You can review, modify, or withdraw your advertising consent at any time directly inside the App by navigating to Settings → Privacy Policy → Ad Consent Settings.

4. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA) & US State Privacy Laws

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), and similar US state privacy laws, residents have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising:

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings: open Settings → Google → All services → Ads and select "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization".
  • You may also manage your ad consent choices inside the App under Settings → Privacy Policy when applicable.

5. Data Retention & How to Delete Your Data

  • On-Device Game Data Deletion: Because all game progress and settings are stored locally on your device, you have full control to delete them at any time:
    1. Inside the App: Open Settings → Reset Progress and confirm to erase all saved stars, level history, and daily streaks immediately.
    2. Via Android OS: Go to Settings → Apps → Block Architect → Storage & cache → Clear storage / Clear data, or simply uninstall the Application.
  • Advertising Identifier Deletion: You can reset or permanently delete your Google Advertising ID (AAID) at any time in your Android device under Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained and deleted in accordance with Google's Data Retention Policies.

6. Data Security

All data transmitted between the Application's integrated Google SDKs and Google's servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS (Transport Layer Security) protocols.

7. Device Permissions Used by the App

Block Architect follows the principle of least privilege and requests only the minimum Android permissions required to function:

  • android.permission.INTERNET — Used to load advertisements via Google AdMob and check remote ad configuration.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to check whether an internet connection is available before requesting an ad.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK on Android 13+ devices to access the Google Advertising ID (subject to user settings and consent).

The Application never requests access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos/media, phone state, SMS, or precise GPS location.

8. Children's Privacy

Block Architect is a general-audience logic puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us via support email, please contact us so that we can promptly delete such information.
